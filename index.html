Welcome to Delightful

Delightful Bakery is a family-run business founded by Nomfundo Mngomezulu. With over 10 years of baking experience, we are proud to supply fresh, handcrafted bread and pastries to the local Total Garage in Durban Central. Step inside our cozy artisan bakery, where warm wood paneling, ambient lighting, and the aroma of fresh dough welcome you.

All our products are baked fresh every morning using the finest ingredients.

🌾 Quality Ingredients

We source only the best local ingredients for our breads and pastries.
