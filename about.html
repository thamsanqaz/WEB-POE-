About Delightful Bakery

Our Story

Delightful Bakery was founded by Nomfundo Mngomezulu, a passionate baker with over 10 years of experience in the culinary industry. What started as a small home-based kitchen has grown into a trusted local brand known for its high-quality breads, pastries, and confectionery.

Nomfundo has spent a decade perfecting her recipes, using only the finest, freshest ingredients to create products that bring comfort and joy to her customers. Today, Delightful Bakery is proud to supply the local Total Garage in Durban Central with fresh baked goods.

🎯 Our Mission

To craft delicious, high-quality baked goods using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients, while delivering exceptional service and convenience to our community and commercial partners.

🌟 Our Vision

To become the preferred local bakery supplier for convenience stores and small businesses across the region, recognized for our commitment to quality, consistency, and community well-being.

👩‍🍳 Meet the Founder

Nomfundo Mngomezulu

With over 10 years of baking experience, Nomfundo is the heart and soul behind Delightful Bakery. Her passion for baking and commitment to quality drives everything we do.
